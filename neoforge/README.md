# illusioner fixes (neoforge 1.21.1)

addon for fqkeh's the illusioner (1.2.0+). not made by fqkeh or lukidonu. if either of you wants it taken down, i will gladly do it.

## fixes

- the arena stays loaded during the fight
- corpses from the corpse mod stay with you in the arena and come back home with you
- the basement uses the same wood as the mansion (regions unexplored)
- mansions in regions unexplored biomes get the right wood
- the basement spawns under luki's woodland mansions
- the arena floor looks right with sodium / iris
- no xaero death waypoints in the void

## build

put the jars from build.gradle in `libs/` and run `./gradlew build` (java 21).
